Multilingual sites: forms answer, and their emails are sent, in the language of the page the form was sent from. Nothing changes on a site without Polylang or WPML.

## Multilingual sites (Polylang)

- **Forms answer in the page language**: validation and error messages come back in the language of the page the visitor is on.
- **Emails are sent in the page language** when the email template has a **published** translation in that language. Without one (no translation yet, or a draft), the email of the default language is sent, as before.
- **Placeholders are checked**: a translation must keep the same placeholders as the original template (`[fullname*]` stays `[fullname*]`, `[phone]` does not become `[phone tel]`; only the text around it is translated). If a translation lost, renamed or retyped one, the default-language email is sent instead, so no visitor data goes missing.
- **Email templates are translated like any post**: they appear as translatable in Polylang (Languages → Settings → Custom post types) and get the usual "+" buttons to add a translation. A new translation copies the key, recipients and "save the messages" setting; you only translate the subject and content. Keep Languages → Settings → Synchronisation → "Custom fields" off, or Polylang would copy one language's subject and content over the other.
- **Translate the success message** once in Languages → Translations.
- **New placeholder `[submissionLanguage]`**: the language of the page the form was sent from (for example "English"), to tell your team which language to reply in. Empty on a single-language site. It is listed under the email content, in "Placeholder syntax reference".
- **Received messages show their language**: a "Language" column in the list and a line in the message detail, on multilingual sites only. "Send the email again" uses the language the message was first sent in.
- **AI translation**: with Polylang AI Translate, placeholders of email templates are protected and never translated.
- WPML: the plugin now declares its translatable content in a `wpml-config.xml` file, also read by WPML.

## Before you translate

Email templates created before this version have no language yet. They keep working as they are, but set their language once in Polylang before adding translations: Polylang offers to assign the default language to content without one (Languages screen), or set it when editing each template.

## Updating

From the WordPress admin: Plugins → update "LCMT Mailer". The received messages table gets a new column automatically on the first page load after the update.
