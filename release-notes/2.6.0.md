Confirmation pages: a form can now take visitors to a page of your choice once their message is sent, instead of showing the success message. Nothing changes for forms without one.

## Confirmation page

- **New setting on each email template**: "Confirmation page", under "Received messages" in the template's edit screen. Pick one of your published pages, or leave it on **None** (the default) to keep the success message.
- **After a successful send**, the visitor is taken to that page. A failed send or a validation error is shown on the form as before, and the visitor stays on it.
- **Multilingual sites (Polylang)**: set the page on the default-language template; visitors go to its published translation in the language of the page they sent the form from, or to the page itself when there is no translation. The setting is copied to new translations of a template.
- **If the page is unpublished or deleted later**, visitors simply see the success message again, and the edit screen points it out.
- **Safe by design**: the plugin only ever sends visitors to a page of your own site, so the form endpoint cannot be used to redirect them elsewhere.

## For developers

- The REST success answer has a new `redirect` key holding the page URL when the form has a confirmation page: `{ "success": true, "message": "…", "redirect": "https://…" }`. Without one, the answer is unchanged.
- New filter `lcmt_mailer_confirmation_url` (`$url, $key, $post, $pageId`) to change the URL, remove it (`''`) or add one to a form without a confirmation page. Whatever it returns is still checked to be on the site.
- New `lcmt:success` event: dispatched on the `<form>` after every successful submission, before the redirect or the success message. It bubbles, carries the REST answer in `detail`, and `preventDefault()` keeps the visitor on the page. Handy for analytics.
- The setting is stored in the `_lcmt_mail_confirmation_page` post meta.

## Updating

From the WordPress admin: Plugins → update "LCMT Mailer". Then open the email templates that should use a confirmation page and pick it.
