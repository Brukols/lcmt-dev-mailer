Performance release: pages showing a form read the plugin's settings with fewer database queries.

## Changed

- **Fewer queries on public pages.** The alert colours (success and error backgrounds and texts, under Email templates → Settings) were each read with a database query of their own on every public page, four in all. They are now fetched in the same single query as the spam protection and attribution settings. Forms look and behave exactly as before.

## Updating

From the WordPress admin: Plugins → update "LCMT Mailer". Nothing to configure.
