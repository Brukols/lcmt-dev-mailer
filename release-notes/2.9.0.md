Each form can now have its own success message, dates read in your site's format in the mails, and two form bugs are fixed: an unticked checkbox was sent as ticked, and some messages showed in English on French sites.

## What changes

- **A success message per form.** Each email template has a new, optional **Success message** field, above **Confirmation page**: the text visitors read after sending this form. Left empty, the form shows your site's message (**Email templates → Settings**), which now applies to the forms without a message of their own. On a multilingual site, the message is translated with the template, like its subject and content.
- **Dates in your site's format.** A `date` field used to arrive in the mail as `2026-12-12`. It now follows your site's date format (**Settings → General**), for example *12 December 2026*, with month names in the site's language. Received messages keep the date as it was sent.
- **Fixed: an unticked checkbox was sent as ticked.** A required checkbox, such as a consent box, did not stop a form from being sent when left unticked, and the mail showed it as ticked. An unticked box now counts as empty: a required one blocks the form, and the server refuses it too. Radio buttons only send the checked one.
- **Fixed: messages in English on a translated site.** When the theme loads the plugin's script itself (to get its stylesheet into the page head), the form's own messages, such as *Please fill in all required fields.*, stayed in English, and the page language was not sent with the form. They now always come with the script.

## Updating

From the WordPress admin: Plugins → update "LCMT Mailer". Nothing to set up: forms without a success message of their own keep showing your site's message.

If you use a page cache, purge it after updating so that pages load the fixed form script.

## For developers

- New post meta `_lcmt_mail_success_message` (`MetaFields::FIELD_SUCCESS_MESSAGE`), translated with the mail in `wpml-config.xml`.
- The `lcmt_mailer_success_message` filter now also receives the form key and the mail post: `function (string $message, string $key, ?\WP_Post $post)`. Existing callbacks with one argument keep working.
- New filter `lcmt_mailer_date_format` (`string $format, \WP_Post $post`) to show the dates of the mails in another format, e.g. `'d/m/Y'`.
- The script's settings (`lcmtMailerFront`) are attached when the script is registered, so they are printed whichever way it is enqueued.
